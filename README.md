# Patent Gap AI – Intelligent Patent Monitoring & Investigation

## Overview

Patent Gap AI is an AI-powered platform designed to automate and enhance patent monitoring and infringement investigation. The project leverages Large Language Models (LLMs), AI agents, and intelligent research workflows to analyze patent claims, discover relevant products and technologies, identify potential technical overlaps, and collect traceable supporting evidence.

The goal is to transform traditional, time-consuming patent investigation into a scalable, intelligent, and evidence-driven process that supports patent professionals in identifying potential infringement opportunities and making informed decisions.

## Problem Statement

Traditional patent monitoring and infringement investigation involve extensive manual research, technical document analysis, product discovery, and evidence collection.

These processes are often:
- Time-consuming and resource-intensive.
- Difficult to scale across large patent portfolios.
- Dependent on manual searches across multiple information sources.
- Challenging when comparing complex patent claims with real-world products.
- Prone to delayed identification of potential infringement opportunities.

Patent Gap AI aims to address these challenges through AI-driven automation and continuous technology monitoring.

## Project Objectives

- Automate patent claim analysis and technical feature extraction.
- Develop AI agents for intelligent product discovery and research.
- Identify potential technical overlaps between patent claims and product specifications.
- Collect and organize supporting evidence from public information sources.
- Generate structured investigation summaries with traceable source references.
- Identify missing evidence and areas requiring further investigation.
- Integrate specialized AI agents into a coordinated investigation workflow.
- Evaluate the accuracy, reliability, and effectiveness of AI-generated findings.

## System Architecture

The platform follows a modular, multi-agent approach in which specialized AI components collaborate to perform patent investigations.

### Core Components

**1. Patent Claim Analysis**
- Analyze patent documents and claims.
- Extract key technical features and claim elements.
- Convert unstructured patent information into structured representations.
- Support technical claim-to-product matching.

**2. AI-Powered Product Discovery**
- Search for relevant products, technologies, and technical specifications.
- Retrieve information from public sources.
- Extract relevant technical descriptions.
- Identify and organize supporting evidence with source links.

**3. Evidence Collection and Technical Matching**
- Compare extracted patent claim elements with product features.
- Identify potential technical overlaps.
- Associate findings with supporting sources.
- Highlight missing, uncertain, or insufficient evidence.

**4. Intelligent Investigation and Multi-Agent Coordination**
- Design and implement specialized AI agents.
- Develop workflows for agent communication and collaboration.
- Integrate AI components through APIs and structured data exchange.
- Synthesize investigation findings into structured reports.
- Support end-to-end testing, evaluation, and documentation.

## Human-in-the-Loop Approach

Patent Gap AI is designed to support, not replace, patent attorneys and intellectual property professionals.

AI-generated findings are intended to assist technical investigation and evidence organization. Legal interpretation, validation, and enforcement decisions remain the responsibility of qualified professionals.
